Data background

  • Mountain Studies Institute (MSI) in Durango/Silverton conducted this study

  • Sampled streams throughout the Bonita Peak Mining District located in San Juan County, CO

    • USEPA website

    • CDPHE website

  • Samples included benthic macroinvertebrates as well as water quality data

  • We will look at the macroinvertebrate data here

  • The water quality data will be incorporated in data module 2

Data structure

  • Each row is the information for one taxon

  • Includes the stream name and specific site information as well as a unique site_id

  • There are columns for the complete taxonomic information for each taxon: phylum, class, order, family, and genus.

    • When information is unknown it appears as an NA which means “Not Available”

  • The taxon column includes the lowest taxonomic information available for that group, typically a Genus name.

  • When the species information is known, the Genus and specific epithet are separated with an underscore

    • i.e., Drunella doddsii appears as” Drunella_doddssii

  • There is also information on the habit ’s and FFG ’s for each taxon.

Shannon’s Diversity Index \(H^\prime\)

\[ H^\prime = - \Sigma \, p_i \, \ln(p_i) \]

Where \(p_i\) is the proportion of taxon \(i\)

shannon <- burrows |> 
  select(stream, taxon, count, total_count) |>
  mutate(pi = count / total_count,
         ln_pi = log(pi),
         pi_ln_pi = pi*ln_pi) |>
  group_by(stream) |>
  summarize(H = -sum(pi_ln_pi))
